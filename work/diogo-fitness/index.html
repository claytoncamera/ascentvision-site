Client work

Diogo Fitness

A coaching website for an online fitness coach based in South Florida, built to explain how coaching works and to make it easy to start a conversation.

The client

Diogo Fitness (Diogo Fit) is one-to-one online fitness coaching from Diogo Camera, based in South Florida, for clients anywhere in the world. We began working with Diogo in October 2026.

What the website is for

A person looking for an online coach wants three things settled: how it works, whether it fits their week, and how to start. The site answers those in plain language and ends in a short application that takes about two minutes, by Instagram, email or form.

What we built

  • More than 250 pages live: a page for every state, the biggest metros and the cities inside them, each written for people in that place. The coach's own region has its page on online fitness coaching in South Florida.
  • Training guides with their research sources on the page, starting with the guide to what online fitness coaching is.
  • Pages for the people coaching suits: beginners, women, men, people over 40 and strength training.
  • A site built so search engines and AI assistants can read every page.
  • Only facts the coach can stand behind: no invented testimonials, results or health claims.

What we do not claim

We publish no results for this site yet. Rankings, visits and new clients take time, and nobody can promise them. We will report them here once there are records to show them.

How we work with Diogo

Diogo owns his name, photos, video and his relationship with his clients. We build and run the website. Our fee for this work depends on the new clients the site brings in, so we have a direct stake in how well it works, and you should know that when you read this page.
