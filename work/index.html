Diogo Fitness

Client since October 2026 · diogofit.com

Diogo Fitness (Diogo Fit) is one-to-one online fitness coaching from Diogo Camera, based in South Florida, for clients anywhere in the world. Ascent Vision built and runs its website: a page for every state and the biggest metros, and a library of training guides. See the guide to what online fitness coaching is.

  • More than 160 pages live: every state, the biggest metros and the cities inside them
  • Training guides with their research sources on the page
  • Built so search engines and AI assistants can read every page
  • An application that takes about two minutes, by Instagram, email or form
