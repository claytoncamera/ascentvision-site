Marketing, lead and AI-answer terms, in plain words

Short definitions of the words a service business owner meets when they deal with marketing, leads, websites and AI answers. Each one links to the answer on this site that explains it in full.

This page is for owners, not marketers: each term is defined by what it means for your business, in plain words, and links to the answer on this site that covers it in full. Where a definition quotes a platform's page or names a government rule, that page is listed under Sources. The other entries summarize the answer they link to, which lists its own sources. This is a plain summary, not legal advice. Ascent Vision keeps the list to terms that one of its answers explains.

A

Accessibility (website)
A website is accessible when people with disabilities can use it, including people who are blind, deaf or cannot use a mouse. The W3C's Web Accessibility Initiative describes accessible sites as "designed and developed so that people with disabilities can use them".

What is website accessibility, and why does it matter for a small business?

Agency (marketing agency)
An outside company paid to bring a business more customers through its website, its visibility on Google and in AI answers, ads, social media or the handling of new inquiries. What matters to an owner is what it hands over: a plan in plain words, a dated record of the work, accounts in the business's name and a monthly count of inquiries by source.

What does a marketing agency actually do for a small business?

AI agent
Software built on a large language model that decides its own steps and uses tools to reach a goal, instead of following a fixed script. Vendors apply the word to many products, so ask what a tool actually does, and keep a person on anything promised to a customer in your name.

What is an AI agent, and should a small business use one?

AI answer
A written reply from an assistant such as ChatGPT, Perplexity or Google's AI Mode to a question like who is a good electrician nearby, which often names a few businesses. It is one of the eight lead channels, and an assistant can only describe a business accurately if it can find clear information about what it does, where and for whom.

How do customers find businesses through AI assistants?

AI assistant
Software you ask questions in plain words and that replies in writing, such as ChatGPT. For a service business, a growing share of customers may ask one before they search or call anyone.

How many people use AI assistants like ChatGPT?

AI browser
A web browser with an AI assistant built in, so a person can ask questions about the pages they have open, compare them and, in some, hand over tasks. Perplexity's Comet, Gemini in Chrome and Copilot in Edge are examples; for a business it may mean a customer compares your site with others in one request.

What are AI browsers, and do they change how customers find a business?

AI Mode
A conversational way to search on Google, separate from the usual list of links. Google's guide for site owners says it suits questions that need more exploration, reasoning or comparison, and that its replies include links to supporting websites.

What are Google's AI Overviews and AI Mode?

AI Overview
A short AI-written summary that Google shows at the top of some search results. Google calls it "an AI-generated snapshot with key information and links to dig deeper" and notes that AI responses may include mistakes.

What are Google's AI Overviews and AI Mode?

AI receptionist
Software that answers a business's phone at once, at any hour, takes the caller's details and can book a visit. It can say wrong things in a business's name, so pick one you can check, and make sure every call lands in your lead list with its source.

Should a small business use an answering service or an AI receptionist?

Answer engine optimization (AEO)
One of several names, alongside GEO and AI SEO, for the same aim: a business being visible and accurately described in answers written by AI. The names have no agreed definitions, so what a seller will check and report tells you more than the label.

Are AEO, GEO and AI SEO the same thing?

Answering service
A company whose staff answer a business's calls when the owner cannot, take a message or the caller's details and pass them on. A human service usually handles unusual or upset callers better than software does.

Should a small business use an answering service or an AI receptionist?

API (application programming interface)
A way for two programs to pass information to each other under rules the provider sets. It usually decides what can be connected: a tool with no API, or a plan that leaves it out, can mean copying data between systems by hand.

What is an API, and why does it matter to a business owner?

Ask Maps
A conversational feature in Google Maps, built with Google's Gemini models, that answers detailed questions about places with a written reply and a customized map. Google announced it on March 12, 2026, rolling out in the U.S. and India, so a business's Maps listing and reviews are likely part of what it can say.

What is Ask Maps in Google Maps, and what does it mean for a local business?

Attribution (lead attribution)
Writing down where each inquiry came from, such as a search, an AI answer, social media, a referral, an ad or an email, and following it to won, lost or still open. It lets you see which marketing brings customers instead of guessing.

What is lead attribution, and why does a small business need it?

Automation (rules-based)
Steps that follow fixed rules you set: when this happens, do that. Usually cheaper, more predictable and easier to check than AI, which can interpret messy input such as a free-written message but can be wrong in new ways. Many useful systems combine the two.

What is the difference between automation and AI?

B

Backup (the 3-2-1 rule)
Copies of whatever your business could not easily recreate: customer records, accounting, email, documents and tool settings, not only the website. The 3-2-1 rule, which CISA calls a trusted guideline, means 3 copies, on 2 types of storage, with 1 off-site, run automatically and tested by restoring.

How should a small business back up its data?

Boosted post
A paid ad made from something you already posted on Facebook or Instagram. It is still an ad: it often suits getting a strong post seen nearby, and it is judged by the inquiries and jobs it brings, not by likes.

Should you boost a Facebook post or run an ad?

Bounce rate
In website analytics, the share of visits that ended without the visitor engaging, such as leaving after one page. A service business should not worry about a high number by itself, because a one-page visit that ends in a phone call can be a success; judge pages by the inquiries they bring.

What is a bounce rate, and should a service business worry about it?

Brand awareness
How many people in your area know and remember your name. It feeds word of mouth, helps in a new area and can make other channels work a little better, and it shows up as more people coming to you by name.

Should your marketing build brand awareness or bring in leads?

Brand style guide
A short set of rules for how your business looks and sounds: the logo files and where each goes, your colors, your fonts, how the name is written and the tone you use. One page is usually enough for a small business.

What is a brand style guide, and does a small business need one?

C

Call to action
The button or line that tells a visitor what to do next, such as "Call for a quote" or "Book a visit". It works best when it asks for one step, starts with a plain verb, says what happens next and uses the same wording wherever it appears.

What is a call to action, and what makes a good one?

Call tracking
Recording every call that is a new inquiry in the same place as your other leads, with who called, what they wanted, where they heard about you and what happened next. Asking how they heard about you on every call, and writing the answer down at the time, is the habit that makes it work; calls are recorded only with the caller's consent.

How do you track leads that come in by phone?

Campaign link (tagged link)
A normal web address with short labels added at the end that show where the link was placed, such as in an email or an ad. Clicks on it let your website's analytics show which campaign sent each visit, but analytics shows visits, not customers, so also note the campaign on each lead.

What is a tagged campaign link, and how do you use one?

Channel (lead channel)
The kind of source a lead is credited to. The eight channels are search, AI answer, social, referral, paid, email, campaign and direct, and every lead carries one so you can see which ones bring jobs.

How can I generate more leads for my service business?

Chatbot
Software on a website that answers simple questions and takes a visitor's details, out of hours too. A bad one gets in the way, gives wrong answers in your name and loses leads, so for many small businesses a short form and a fast human reply are enough.

Should a small business use a chatbot on its website?

CMS (content management system)
The part of a website you log into to change words, photos and pages without writing code. Website builders have one built in; WordPress is a free, open-source one installed on hosting you choose. Plugins are add-ons that each need updates and someone responsible for them.

What is a CMS, and what are plugins on a website?

Consent (calls and texts)
A person's permission to be called or texted. Text or call a lead or a past customer only with their consent, stop as soon as they ask, and ask separately before sending any offer, because answering what someone asked about is different from marketing to them. This is a plain summary, not legal advice.

Can you text a new lead or a past customer, and what consent do you need?

Contact form
The form on a website where a visitor sends an inquiry. It should ask only what you need to reply well, a name, one way to reach them and what they need help with, because every extra field costs completed forms.

What should a contact form ask?

Conversion rate
The share of a site's visitors who do what you hoped, which for a service business is usually to get in touch. If a thousand people visit and twenty send an inquiry, the conversion rate is two percent; a clearer, faster and easier-to-use site tends to raise it.

What is a conversion rate, and how do you improve it on a service website?

Core update (Google)
A broad change Google makes to its search systems several times a year. Google says core updates do not target specific sites or pages, so a drop afterward is usually not a penalty; check inquiries rather than rankings and wait for the update to finish before changing anything.

What is a Google core update, and what should an owner do after one?

Cost per click
The charge each time someone clicks your ad. A click is a tap on the ad, which is not the same as a visit or a customer, so whether ads pay shows up in the cost per inquiry and per job won instead.

What do cost per click, impressions and reach mean in an ad report?

Cost per lead (cost per inquiry)
What the ads cost in a month divided by the inquiries they brought. It only means something set beside the cost per job won and what a customer is worth to you, which together tell you whether that cost is affordable.

What do cost per click, impressions and reach mean in an ad report?

CRM
A customer relationship management system: one shared place that records every customer and inquiry, who they are, how they found you, what was said and what happens next. A well-kept spreadsheet often works at first; software earns its place when inquiries start slipping through or more than one person handles leads.

What is a CRM, and does a small business need one?

Custom software
Software built around one business's own way of working, such as a dashboard, a portal, an internal tool or an app. Worth considering when your own process is what sets you apart, when you are bending your work around a bought tool, or when nothing connects the pieces you rely on.

Should you buy off-the-shelf software or have something built?

Customer onboarding
The steps between a customer saying yes and the first visit: a welcome message that says what happens next, a short intake form, the agreement sent for signature, a booking link, a deposit invoice if you take one, and reminders. Many can run by themselves; a person stays on anything that needs judgment.

How do you automate the first steps with a new customer?

Customer portal
A login page where each customer sees their own jobs, quotes, invoices, documents or bookings. Many small businesses do not need to build one: a few emailed links may do, and some business software already includes one.

What is a customer portal, and does a small business need one?

Customer value (lifetime value)
What an average customer brings in over a year, or over the whole time they stay with you, minus what it costs to serve them. That figure tells you how much you can sensibly spend to win one.

How do you work out what a customer is worth to your business?

D

Dashboard (operations dashboard)
One screen that shows what came in, what is being done, what was won and what is owed: new inquiries and their sources, how fast you replied, jobs booked and in progress, outcomes, money invoiced and unpaid, and how much capacity is left.

What should a small business dashboard show?

Data breach
An event in which customer or business data is exposed. The response is to secure systems without destroying evidence, find out what was exposed and tell the people who need to know, with notice deadlines set by the law that applies. This is a plain summary, not legal advice.

What should a small business do after a data breach?

Direct mail (flyers and door hangers)
Flyers, door hangers and postcards sent or left at homes, reaching people whether or not they are searching. It tends to build familiarity more than it catches urgent needs, so give each drop its own phone number, short web address or code and judge it by jobs won, not pieces sent.

Do door hangers, flyers and direct mail still work for a service business?

Direct (traffic and leads)
In website analytics, direct traffic is the channel for visits that arrive by a saved link or a typed address, and it can include people who heard your name from a person or an AI answer and then typed it. In lead records, direct is the channel for a customer who typed your address or came back on their own, and a returning customer counts as direct.

What does 'direct' traffic mean in your website analytics?

Domain authority
A score from a third-party SEO tool, not from Google, that estimates how likely a website is to rank. A higher score is not a business result: judge search work by the inquiries and customers that arrive from it.

What is domain authority, and should you pay an agency to raise it?

Domain (domain name)
Your web address. It is separate from hosting, the rented space where the site's files live, and from the website itself, and the business should control all three accounts.

What is the difference between a domain, hosting and a website, and how do you find out who hosts yours?

Domain privacy
A registrar option that keeps your contact details off the public registration record for a domain. Check that the business stays the registered owner, because some versions can put the provider's name on the record instead.

Should you turn on domain privacy for your business domain?

Drip campaign
A short series of emails or texts sent on a schedule after someone does something, such as asking for a quote or booking a first job. Each should stop when the person replies or asks to stop, and texts go only with the person's consent.

What is a drip campaign, and does a small service business need one?

E

Electronic signature
A signature given electronically. Federal law and Florida's law generally give it the same effect as a signature in ink for most documents, with some exceptions such as wills. Get a clear yes to sign electronically and keep the signed copy and a record of who signed and when. This is a plain summary, not legal advice.

Are electronic signatures legally binding for a small business?

Email list
The people who agreed to hear from you by email, usually past customers and people who asked about your services. A bought list is a different thing: the people on it never asked to hear from you, which is why buying one usually backfires.

Should a small business buy an email list?

Email marketing (newsletter)
Sending short, useful emails to people who already know you, to stay in mind for the next job and for referrals. It works poorly for reaching strangers; keep each message honest, about one thing and easy to unsubscribe from.

Is email marketing worth it for a small service business?

Estimate (free estimate)
A visit or call to price a job before the customer commits. Free estimates can bring in more inquiries, especially where customers expect them, but each visit to a poor fit costs unpaid time; a short screen of questions first, or a paid assessment credited to the job, are common middle grounds.

Should a service business offer free estimates?

F

Fake review
A review that is not based on a real experience, or that misrepresents who wrote it. Google's policy bars soliciting or encouraging reviews that do not represent a genuine experience, and the FTC's rule (16 CFR 465.2) treats a business writing, creating or selling a review that materially misrepresents the reviewer or their experience as an unfair or deceptive practice. This is a plain summary, not legal advice.

Can you get a fake or unfair Google review taken down?

Favicon
The small icon that shows in a browser tab and, on Google, can appear beside a site's name in search results. A simple square version of your logo, or your initials in your main color, usually works best because it has to read when tiny.

What is a favicon, and does a small business website need one?

Fixed price vs hourly (software)
Two ways to pay for a build. Fixed price suits work that can be described clearly before it starts; hourly suits work still taking shape. A vague brief at a fixed price and hourly work with no limit can each go wrong; a fixed price per stage is a common middle route.

Fixed price or hourly: which suits a software project?

Follow-up
The short, useful messages sent to a lead who has gone quiet, spaced out over a couple of weeks, then stopped with the outcome recorded. Each one should make it easy to say yes, no or not now.

How should you follow up with a lead that goes quiet?

Fractional CTO
An experienced technology leader who works for a business part time instead of as a full-time hire: making technology choices, reviewing quotes and plans and overseeing the people who build, rather than building everything personally.

What is a fractional CTO, and does a small business need one?

Fractional marketing lead
An experienced marketer hired part time to set the plan and oversee the work, rather than a full-time employee or an agency. It often suits a business that needs direction more than extra hands.

Should you hire a marketing employee, a fractional marketing lead or an agency?

Freelancer
One skilled person you deal with directly, usually with lower overheads than an agency but less cover when they are away. The questions that matter are the same either way: who owns the accounts, and what gets reported.

Should you hire a freelancer or an agency for SEO and marketing?

Funnel (marketing funnel)
A way to picture the steps people go through before contacting a business: they notice it, compare it with others, and some get in touch. A local service business often has a short one, sometimes a single search and a call, so the useful move is to find where people drop out and fix that step first.

What is a marketing funnel, and does a small service business need one?

G

GEO audit (AI search audit)
A review of what AI assistants say about a business: the questions asked, which assistants answered, the date and location, every answer kept in full, the errors and the pages behind them, the competitors named, a starting count of AI-answer inquiries and a short ranked list of fixes.

What should a GEO or AI search audit include?

GEO (generative engine optimization)
The work of getting a business named, described correctly and recommended inside the answers AI systems write, such as those from ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google's AI Overviews and AI Mode. SEO aims for a ranked link on a results page; GEO aims for a mention inside the answer itself.

What is GEO (generative engine optimization)?

Google Business Profile
The listing Google shows for a business on Search and Maps, with its name, hours, contact details, photos, reviews and a short description. Google describes it as a free Business Profile the business takes control of; for most local service businesses it is worth claiming.

What is a Google Business Profile, and does a service business need one?

Google Local Guide
A member of Google's program for people who add reviews, photos, edits and facts to Google Maps. Treat a guide's review like any other, and never offer a guide anything for one: Google's review policy bars offering incentives in exchange for posting a review.

What is a Google Local Guide, and do their reviews count for more?

Google Local Services Ads
Ads in Google Search for certain local service categories where, in Google's words, a business will "only pay when a customer gets in touch from your ad". Google screens the businesses that take part, and the ads are best judged by booked jobs, not by lead count.

What are Google Local Services Ads, and are they worth it?

Google Partner
A badge Google gives to companies that meet its Google Ads requirements for performance, ad spend and certifications. It speaks to paid-ads activity; by itself it does not show skill with free search results, map listings or AI answers.

What does it mean when an agency says it is a Google Partner?

H

Hallucination
An AI assistant stating something wrong, outdated or made up about a business, such as an old phone number, a service you stopped offering or a location you closed. You cannot edit an assistant's answer, so check what assistants say about you and keep your own website plain and current.

Do AI assistants get facts about businesses wrong?

Hand-over (software)
What a developer leaves with you when a project ends: the working software plus a written pack saying where the code lives, who owns each account, how to run and restore it, which outside services it relies on and when they renew, how changes were tested and who to contact.

What should a developer hand over when the project ends or they leave?

Hosting
The rented computer space where a website's files are kept. Look for an account in the business's name, backups you can actually restore, support you can reach when the site is down, secure connections, fast pages and renewal terms in plain words.

What should a small business look for in website hosting?

HTTPS
The secure version of a web address, which keeps what a visitor's browser and your site send each other private and harder to tamper with. It matters most on pages where people type in their details, such as a contact form.

Does a small business website need HTTPS?

I

Impressions and reach
An impression is one showing of an ad; reach is how many different people or accounts saw it at least once. Both describe attention, not inquiries or customers.

What do cost per click, impressions and reach mean in an ad report?

Inquiry
A person getting in touch about work: a call, a form, a message or a booking. Answer it quickly, with a reply that thanks the person, answers what they asked and offers one clear next step.

How do you write a good first reply to a new inquiry?

Inventory tracking
Counting what counts as stock on a regular schedule, writing every count in one place and setting a reorder point for items that would stall you. A spreadsheet is often enough for one location and one person; software earns its place with several places or several people.

How should a small business keep track of its inventory?

L

Landing page
A web page made for one campaign, such as an ad or a flyer, that picks up the offer the visitor responded to and asks them to take a single step. A business needs one only when it is sending people to a specific offer; otherwise a clear homepage and service pages usually do.

What is a landing page, and when does a small business need one?

Large language model (LLM)
Software trained on a large body of text to guess which words are likely to come next, so it can write, summarize and answer questions in plain language. It powers chatbots such as ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini, and because it predicts wording rather than looking facts up, it can sound sure and still be wrong.

What is a large language model (LLM), in plain terms?

Lead
A person who has shown interest in your work and can be contacted, which for a service business usually means an inquiry. Each lead is tagged with its source and followed to an outcome of won, lost or still open.

How can I generate more leads for my service business?

Lead ads
Ads on Facebook or Instagram that open a form inside the app, so a person can send their details without visiting your website. They can bring many forms from people who were only mildly interested, so record each lead as paid and judge the ads by jobs won.

Are Facebook and Instagram lead ads worth it for a service business?

Lead generation company
A business that finds people who want a service and sends their details or calls to the businesses that provide it, usually charging per lead. Before you sign, ask who else receives each lead, what counts as billable and whether the customer ends up dealing with you or with the seller.

What does a lead generation company do, and should you pay per lead?

Lead magnet
Something free a business offers in return for a visitor's contact details, such as a checklist, a short buying guide or a free estimate. It tends to help when customers research for weeks and matters less when most jobs are urgent; send marketing email only with the person's consent.

What is a lead magnet, and does a service business need one?

Lead site
A marketplace such as Angi or Thumbtack that sends inquiries to service businesses for a fee, often to several businesses at once. Judge each site by the jobs you win from it, and keep every customer's details in your own lead list, not only inside the site's app.

Should you buy leads from sites like Angi or Thumbtack?

Lead system
How an inquiry travels from first contact to a yes or a no: where it lands, who is told, how quickly it gets a reply, where it is written down and which source gets the credit. Any business that takes inquiries has one already; what matters is whether customers slip away along the way.

What is a lead system, and does a small business need one?

Lead tracker
A single shared sheet or board with one row per inquiry, holding the date, name, contact details, what they need, source channel, first reply time, outcome and notes. It only works if every inquiry from every channel is added the day it arrives.

How do you set up a simple lead tracker?

Local SEO
Being found by people who search for a service in a particular place, such as a plumber in a named town or a tax preparer near me. It matters most to businesses whose customers are nearby, whether customers visit them or they travel to customers.

What is local SEO?

M

Managed IT support (managed service provider)
An arrangement where a provider looks after your computers, accounts and software under an ongoing agreement instead of fixing things only when you call: usually updates, backups, user accounts and help requests from staff. The agreement's hours, response and fix times, exclusions and exit terms matter most.

What is managed IT support, and what should it include?

Marketing audit
A look at every way a business wins and serves customers, from its website, Google profile and reviews to social pages, ads, email and how inquiries get answered. A good one gives the owner a plain list of what exists, what works, what is missing and what to do first.

What is a marketing audit, and is it worth paying for?

Marketing calendar
One page that shows the year ahead: the busy and slow months, holidays that change your hours, the seasonal jobs to promote before demand starts, and the dates to check results and renew things such as your domain and licenses.

What is a marketing calendar, and what goes on one for a service business?

Marketing report
A one-page monthly summary that answers four questions: how many inquiries and customers came in and from which sources, what each source cost, what was done and what changed because of it, and what happens next. Traffic and rankings appear only as supporting detail.

What should a marketing report show a small business owner?

Mention (in AI answers)
A reference to a business on a website it does not control, such as local news, a trade or community group, a supplier, a partner or a chamber. Assistants that search the web often draw on such pages, and no outsider can see how much any one mention counts.

Do mentions on other websites help you show up in AI answers?

Mobile app
Software a customer installs on a phone. Most small businesses are better served by a website that works well on phones; an app tends to make sense when customers return often and log in, when it must work without a signal, or when staff work in the field.

Does your business need a mobile app?

Mobile-friendly
A website that is fast, readable and easy to act on from a phone, which is often the first place people look for help. A site that is slow or awkward on a phone loses many visitors before they call.

Does a small business website really need to work well on phones?

Multi-factor authentication (MFA)
Signing in with two or more kinds of proof, such as a password plus a code from an app or a physical key, so a stolen password alone is usually not enough. CISA recommends requiring it wherever possible, starting with admin accounts.

What is multi-factor authentication, and should every business account use it?

MVP (minimum viable product)
The smallest working version of software that does its main job for real users. Usually a sound way to start a build: get one core task working, learn from real use, then decide what to add, without leaving out security, backups or ownership of your own data.

What is an MVP, and should a small business start with one?

N

Near-me search
A search for a service plus the words near me or a town name. Customers usually search the service and the place, not your business name, so a page that names both in plain words matters.

Why isn't my business showing up on Google?

No-code and low-code tools
Tools that let someone build forms, simple workflows and internal lists by arranging pieces on a screen, with little or no code. Often enough for a first version or a task with few users and standard rules; check data export, account ownership and the limits before you start.

Can you build it with no-code tools, or do you need custom software?

No-show
A customer who booked an estimate or an appointment and did not turn up. Written confirmation, a reminder the day before (by text only with the person's consent) and one-step rescheduling cut them, and recording each no-show with the lead's source shows whether some channels bring more.

How do you cut no-shows for estimates and appointments?

O

Offline conversion tracking
Telling an ad account which of the leads it sent later became paying jobs, so its reports count customers rather than form fills and calls. Worth doing once every lead already carries its source and an outcome.

What is offline conversion tracking, and should a service business use it?

Off-the-shelf software
Software you buy ready-made, which the vendor keeps working. Usually the first thing to try when it can do most of the job; a bought product can also show you what you actually need before anything is built.

Should you buy off-the-shelf software or have something built?

Online booking
Letting customers pick a time on your website without waiting for a reply. It suits visits with a fixed length that need no quote first, and each booking should be confirmed and recorded with its source, in booking data the business owns.

Should a service business let customers book online?

Organic search
Visits that come from unpaid links, such as ordinary search results, rather than from ads. In analytics it is a label for visits, not for customers, so line it up with your own lead records with care.

What do 'organic', 'paid' and 'referral' traffic mean in your website analytics?

P

Paid search
Ads that appear with search results and are paid for by the click. They can bring calls within days and stop the moment you stop paying, while SEO usually takes months to build and tends to last after the work slows.

Is paying for Google ads better than SEO for a small business?

Password manager
Software that generates complex passwords, fills them in and stores them securely, so each person remembers one master password. The business, not one employee, should hold the master login, so a leaver cannot take the only copy of its logins.

Does a small business need a password manager?

Pay per click (PPC)
A way of paying for ads in which you pay for each click on the ad. What one click costs is the cost per click.

What do cost per click, impressions and reach mean in an ad report?

Pay per lead
Paying a set fee for each inquiry a company or a lead site sends you. It can work when the leads fit your work, are not sold to several competitors at once and turn into jobs.

What does a lead generation company do, and should you pay per lead?

Pay-for-performance SEO
An arrangement where an agency is paid only when a result is reached, usually a ranking. When payment depends on position, the easiest searches are the natural choice, and those often bring no customers; a better deal ties payment to inquiries you can trace or to jobs won.

Is pay-for-performance SEO a good deal?

Payment processor
The company that takes a card or online payment and passes the money to your bank, for a fee. Ask in writing how every fee is charged, how many days pass before a sale reaches your bank, when money can be held, how disputes work and how you leave.
