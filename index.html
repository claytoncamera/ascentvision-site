What we do

  1. Websites

    Fast, plain-spoken websites built around one action: the call, the application or the booking that pays for the site.

  2. Search and AI answers (SEO and GEO)

    Pages written to rank on Google and to be quoted by ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google's AI answers: one page per real question, each answer sourced and complete on its own.

    Short inquiry and application forms, with every lead tagged by where it came from and handed to a person fast, so you can see which work brings customers in.

Every lead has a source

A search, an AI answer, a referral or an ad: each lead we bring in is recorded with where it came from and followed to an outcome. Nothing is counted twice, and nothing is reported that the records do not show.
