Why the name

Ascent is the climb. The aim is a business that gets steadily stronger: better systems, less wasted time, more customers who found it on their own. That is what we are here to build.

How we spend our time

We work. We do not drink, smoke or party, and we would rather spend the evening building than anywhere else. We use AI every day in our own work and run more than one business between us, so the systems we build for clients are the kind we rely on ourselves. When you work with Ascent Vision you work with the two founders, not a layer of account managers.
