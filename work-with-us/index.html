Apply to work with us

Nine short questions, about two minutes. Only three are required. One of the founders reads every application and replies.

01

What is the business?

The name, and one line on what it does and for whom.

02

Where are your customers?

A city and state, a region, or "anywhere" if you sell online.

03

What do you need?

Pick everything that fits. You can change your mind on the call.

04

How do customers find you today?

Your best guess is fine.

05

Do you have a website?

Paste the address. Skip this if there is none yet.

06

What is the biggest problem right now?

Pick any that sound familiar.

07

When do you want to start?

There is no wrong answer. It only changes what we send first.

08

Anything else we should know?

What you have tried, what you are hoping for, or nothing at all.

09

How do we reach you?

We reply with a few questions first, then book a short call if it looks like a fit.

Best way to reach you (optional)

How did you hear about us? (optional)

We use these details only to reply to you. See the privacy notice.

What happens after you send it

  1. It reaches both founders at once. Every application lands on our board the moment you send it, with an alert to our phones.
  2. You get a reply with questions, not a pitch. Usually about how customers find you now and what a good month looks like.
  3. A short call about fit. If we can help, we say what we would do first and what it costs. If we cannot, we say that too.
