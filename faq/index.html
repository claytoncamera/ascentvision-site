Working with us

What does Ascent Vision do?

Ascent Vision is a Florida marketing company. It makes service businesses visible on Google and in AI answers, builds the lead systems that turn those visits into inquiries, and upgrades or builds the website they run on. Every lead is tracked back to where it came from.

What is GEO?

GEO, generative engine optimization, is the work of making a business the one AI assistants such as ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google's AI answers recommend when a customer asks them who to call. More people now ask an assistant before they search, so being part of that answer matters as much as ranking.

How is GEO different from SEO?

SEO aims for a ranked link on a search results page. GEO aims for a mention or recommendation inside an AI-written answer. The two support each other, and Ascent Vision measures them separately, because a business that ranks is not always the business an assistant recommends.

How do I know what the work produced?

Every lead from an Ascent Vision site or campaign is recorded with where it came from, such as a search, an AI answer, a referral or an ad, so a client can see which leads the work produced and what happened to each one.

Do you publish prices?

No. Every engagement is scoped on a call first, because the work depends on the business, the market and what already exists. Start with the short form on the Work with us page.

Who does Ascent Vision work with?

Ascent Vision works with small businesses and independent professionals who sell a service and want more of their customers to find them through search and AI answers. Its first client is an online fitness coaching business.

Who owns the website and the content?

The client owns their domain, their content and their lead data. Ascent Vision builds and runs the work; it does not hold a client's site or customers hostage.
