Working with us

What does Ascent Vision do?

Ascent Vision is a Florida technology company for businesses and independent professionals. It designs and builds websites, custom software and apps, business systems and AI assistants and automations; makes businesses visible on Google and in AI answers (SEO and GEO); generates leads and tracks every one back to where it came from; and keeps it all running with ongoing technical support. One team, for everything technical.

Is Ascent Vision the same company as Ascent Vision Technologies or CACI Ascent Vision?

No. Ascent Vision LLC is a Florida technology company founded in 2026 by Clayton Camera and Grayson Sorrentino. CACI Ascent Vision, LLC (formerly Ascent Vision Technologies) is a Montana imaging and counter-drone company, owned by CACI International since 2020, that does business as "Ascent Vision" in federal contracting records. The two have never been connected.

Who does Ascent Vision work with?

Ascent Vision works with businesses and independent professionals that need something built, found or kept running: a website, custom software, an AI assistant or automation, more customers from search and AI answers, or one team to call for anything technical. Its first client is an online fitness coaching business.

Is Ascent Vision an agency?

Not in the usual sense. There are no account managers between the client and the work: the two founders, Clayton Camera and Grayson Sorrentino, scope it, build it and answer for it, and every lead it produces is tracked to its source.

How do I know what the work produced?

Every lead from an Ascent Vision site or campaign is recorded with where it came from, such as a search, an AI answer, a referral or an ad, so a client can see which leads the work produced and what happened to each one.

Do you publish prices?

No. Every engagement is scoped on a call first, because the work depends on the business, the market and what already exists. Start with the application on the Work with us page: nine short questions, about two minutes.

Can I meet Ascent Vision in person?

Yes. Most of Ascent Vision's meetings are by video call, and clients near Tallahassee, Florida, can also meet the founders in person by appointment.

Who owns the website, the software and the data?

The client owns their domain, their content, their data and the accounts their website and systems run on, and gets documentation of what was built. Who owns the code of custom software is written into the agreement before work begins. Ascent Vision does not hold a client's site, systems or customers hostage.

Does Ascent Vision work with businesses outside Florida?

Yes. Ascent Vision works with businesses and independent professionals across the United States. Most meetings are by video call, so where the business is does not change the work. Clients near Tallahassee, Florida, can also meet in person by appointment.

What happens after I apply?

Every application reaches both founders the moment it is sent. One of them reads it and replies with a few questions, usually about how customers find you now and what a good month looks like, not a pitch. If it looks like a fit, the next step is a short call.

What we build

Can Ascent Vision build my website?

Yes. Ascent Vision designs and builds websites from scratch, rebuilds sites that cannot be saved and upgrades the ones that can. Every site is built to be fast on a phone, clear about what the business does and set up to turn visitors into inquiries, with booking, payments and forms connected where the business needs them.

Does Ascent Vision build custom software and apps?

Yes. Ascent Vision builds web apps, dashboards, internal tools, customer and partner portals and mobile apps, and connects the systems a business already uses, such as its CRM, booking, payments and email. Each project starts by checking whether something off the shelf already does the job.

Can Ascent Vision build an AI assistant or automate our work?

Yes. Ascent Vision builds AI assistants that answer customers and staff from the business's own information, on a website, by text or by phone, and AI systems and agents that handle repetitive work such as intake, follow-up, quotes and reporting. Each one is set up with written limits on what it may say and do, and a person approves what reaches customers until the business decides otherwise.

Do you support technology you did not build?

Yes, after a review. Ascent Vision looks at the site, domains, email and accounts a business already has, says what needs fixing first and then keeps them running. If something would cost more to keep than to replace, it says so.

Search and AI answers

What is GEO?

GEO, generative engine optimization, is the work of making a business the one AI assistants such as ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google's AI answers recommend when a customer asks them who to call. People now ask assistants as well as search engines, so being part of that answer matters alongside ranking.

How is GEO different from SEO?

SEO aims for a ranked link on a search results page. GEO aims for a mention or recommendation inside an AI-written answer. The two support each other, and Ascent Vision measures them separately, because a business that ranks is not always the business an assistant recommends.

Which AI assistants does Ascent Vision work on?

ChatGPT, Google's Gemini, Perplexity, Microsoft Copilot, Claude, and Google's AI Overviews and AI Mode: the assistants people use when they ask who to call. Each one can describe the same business differently, so what each says is checked and reported on its own, in full.
