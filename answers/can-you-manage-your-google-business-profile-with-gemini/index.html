Can you manage your Google Business Profile with Gemini, and should you?

Ascent Vision · Published October 25, 2026

Yes, for some owners. Google's help page says that once a Business Profile is connected to the Gemini web app, Gemini can help update profile details, draft review replies, create posts, summarize review feedback and show performance metrics. It is limited to people who own or manage only one verified profile. Check anything it changes or posts before relying on it.

What Google says Gemini can do

  • Update the profile, such as business hours, contact info and action links.
  • Engage with customers by drafting replies to reviews and creating posts. The page also lists scheduling, publishing and deleting posts.
  • Analyze and summarize customer feedback from reviews, including themes.
  • Show business insights, such as impressions, website clicks, direction requests and bookings.

Who can use it

The page says you must be an owner or manager of only one verified Business Profile, be 18 or over, be signed in to the Gemini web app with a personal Google Account tied to the profile, and have Keep Activity on. It says the feature is not available to people who have access to more than one verified profile, and that it works only in the Gemini web app at gemini.google.com. A work or school account does not work for now, and owners of several locations are left out.

What to check before anything goes live

The page describes asking Gemini to perform actions, such as updating business hours, and lists publishing posts among them. It lists these actions without describing a confirmation step. As general advice, it suits an owner of one location who wants to change hours or draft a post quickly, since the same edits can be made directly in the profile. Read what Gemini proposes, then open the profile and confirm that hours, links and replies say what you meant. For review replies, the related answer on using AI to reply to reviews explains why a person should check the facts and post them.

What the page does not cover

The page is about managing a profile and reading its insights. Google presents the insights as a way to improve your visibility, but the page does not describe how Google ranks a profile, and it says nothing about what other AI assistants say about a business. For what Gemini is, see the related answer on Google Gemini.
