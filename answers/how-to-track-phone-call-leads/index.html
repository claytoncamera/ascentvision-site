How do you track leads that come in by phone?

Ascent Vision · Published October 15, 2026 · Updated October 27, 2026

Write down every call that is a new inquiry in the same place as your other leads, with who called, what they wanted, where they heard about you and what happened next. Ask "how did you hear about us?" on every call and write the answer down at the time. A consistent habit is a good start for a small business. If you record calls, get the caller's consent first.

What to record for each call

  • Date and time.
  • Who called and how to reach them.
  • What they need.
  • Where they heard about you, in their words.
  • The outcome: won, lost or still open, and whether it was a good fit.

Asking where they heard about you

Ask early, in a natural way: "Before I forget, how did you find us?" Write down the exact answer, then sort it into a channel later: search, AI answer, social, referral, paid, email, campaign or direct (a returning customer counts as direct). Customers do not always remember precisely, so treat the answer as a good guide, not proof. The related answer on "how did you hear about us" options gives wording for each channel.

Not every call is an inquiry

Google's help page on measuring calls from ads, written for advertisers, says a call can be counted as a conversion when it lasts longer than "a minimum duration you set". Even ad measurement does not treat every call as a good one. As general advice, log wrong numbers, sales pitches and spam in a separate column rather than deleting them, so your inquiry count stays honest. The related answer on counting leads from your Google Business Profile explains why a count of button clicks is not a count of calls.

Stop losing calls

  • Log every missed call as a lead the same day, even with no voicemail, and return it the same day.
  • Text a missed caller only with the person's consent, and keep the text to their call.
  • Use a voicemail message that says when you will call back.
  • Keep calls and form inquiries in one list, so nothing is counted twice or missed.
  • Review the list each month next to your other leads.
