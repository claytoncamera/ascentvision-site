What is local SEO?

Ascent Vision · Published October 15, 2026

Local SEO is the work of being found by people searching for a service in a particular place, such as "plumber in Tallahassee" or "tax preparer near me". It matters most to businesses whose customers are nearby: those that customers visit, and those that travel to customers. For a business that serves customers anywhere online, place matters less than the service itself.

Who it matters to

  • Businesses customers visit: a salon, a clinic, a shop.
  • Businesses that travel to customers: a plumber, a cleaner, a mobile mechanic.
  • Less so: businesses that serve customers anywhere by phone or video, where the service matters more than the town.

Where local searches show you

Local searches can show map results and business listings as well as ordinary web results and AI answers. Google's guidelines limit Business Profiles to businesses that make in-person contact with customers: either customers visit a physical location, or the business travels to them. A virtual office, an address the business does not actually operate from, is not eligible.

What a customer needs to see

  • That you serve their area, stated plainly.
  • What you do there, in their words.
  • Recent, real reviews.
  • A fast, easy way to get in touch.

Local is not the whole picture

Customers also ask AI assistants for recommendations in their area, and many find a business through a referral and then check it online. Whatever the route, record where each inquiry came from, so you know which one is worth your time.
