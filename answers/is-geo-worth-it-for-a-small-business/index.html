Is GEO worth it for a small business?

Ascent Vision · Published October 19, 2026

GEO is worth it when your customers are already asking AI assistants who to hire. Three signs decide it: whether your customers skew under 50, the group that used ChatGPT at about twice the rate of older adults in Pew's 2026 survey; whether your jobs are researched in advance rather than booked in an emergency; and whether inquiries that mention an AI answer already reach you. If two of the three hold, the channel is worth working now.

Who is asking assistants

Age is the sharpest divide in Pew Research Center's June 2026 report, from a February 2026 survey: adults under 50 were about twice as likely as those 50 and older to report using ChatGPT, 57% against 28%. The related answer on how many people use AI assistants has the rest of the usage numbers, so they are not repeated here.

Considered jobs versus emergency jobs

Someone with water coming through the ceiling calls the first plumber they can reach; someone planning a kitchen or choosing an accountant has time to ask an assistant to compare options. GEO usually pays off sooner for the considered purchase, where a written recommendation gets read before anyone calls. For emergency trades, search and a fast-answering phone often matter more; the related answer on GEO vs SEO covers how the two fit together.

Check whether AI inquiries are already arriving

Ask every new inquiry how they found you; if a caller says ChatGPT, Gemini or Perplexity suggested you, tag that inquiry as an AI answer rather than lumping it in with search. Then ask the assistants what they say about your business; the related answer on checking what AI says about your business walks through it. A few AI-sourced inquiries a month means the channel already exists; the only question is whether to work it.

A fair expectation

No one can promise that a particular assistant will recommend a particular business; judge GEO by whether assistants mention and describe you correctly and by how many inquiries arrive tagged as an AI answer and are won. If none of the three signs applies yet, put the budget into the website and search first and revisit in six months.
