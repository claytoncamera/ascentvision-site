What does looking after a small business website involve?

Ascent Vision · Published October 15, 2026 · Updated October 27, 2026

Looking after a small business website means renewing the domain and hosting on time, keeping the software and plugins up to date, checking that forms and phone links work, keeping details such as services and hours current, and keeping a backup. Most checks are quick; skip them and problems can go unnoticed for weeks.

Monthly

  • Send a test inquiry through every form and check it arrives.
  • Tap the phone number and email links on a phone.
  • Check the site loads and reads well on a phone.
  • Apply software and plugin updates, if your platform needs them.

Every few months

  • Check services, prices you show, hours, areas and team details are still true.
  • Remove pages about things you no longer offer, or update them.
  • Make sure a recent backup exists and you know how to restore it.

Every year

  • Renew the domain, and keep auto-renew on with a card that will not expire.
  • Check who has access to the domain, hosting and site, and remove anyone who has left.
  • Confirm the domain and accounts are in the business's name.
  • Check that the security certificate, which makes the padlock show, renews on its own.

Updates and backups

On WordPress, the project's own update guide says to "always update WordPress to the latest version" and calls it "a good idea to back up your website" first. Its backup guide says a backup has two parts, the database and the files, and suggests weekly backups for smaller sites and daily for busy ones. On a hosted builder, the provider often handles some of this, so ask which parts it covers.

CISA's small business guidance describes a 3-2-1 rule: three copies of important files, on two kinds of storage, with one kept off-site. It also advises testing that you can restore. The related answer on website hosting covers what to ask a host about backups.

Who should own it

Someone in the business should own the list, even if a provider does the work. The domain renewal and the account access are easy to miss and can be costly when they lapse.
