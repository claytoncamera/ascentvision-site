What are the different parts of a Google results page?

Ascent Vision · Published October 25, 2026

A Google results page mixes paid and earned parts. Ads are paid and labeled, and Google says it shows none for most searches. Map results, AI Overviews and the ordinary text results are earned: Google says no one can buy better placement in its unpaid results, and that there is no way to pay for a better local ranking. Which parts appear, and how they look, varies by search, device and place.

The parts at a glance

PartWhat it isPaid or earned
AdsLabeled ad sections, shown only when Google judges them relevantPaid
Local resultsNearby businesses, on Google Maps and in SearchEarned
AI OverviewsAn AI-written summary with links, on some searchesEarned
Text resultsA result based on a page's text, once called the plain blue linkEarned
Image and video resultsA result based on an image or video on a pageEarned

The paid part

Ads are paid and labeled, and the related answer on whether Google Ads help your free rankings covers how Google separates them from everything else on the page.

The earned parts

Local results: Google's Business Profile help says customers searching for a business near them find local results on Google Maps and Search, and that "There's no way to request or pay for a better local ranking on Google". The related answer on what a Google Business Profile is covers the listing behind them.

AI Overviews: Google's guide for site owners says they are designed to show on searches where they add something beyond what people already get, and that there are no additional requirements to appear in them. The related answer on AI Overviews and AI Mode describes both.

Text results: Google's gallery of results-page parts says a text result was formerly known as a "web result" or "plain blue link". Google's overview of Search adds that it does not accept payment for result rankings.

Why no two pages look the same

Google's gallery says how the parts look can change over time, and that a result can look different on a desktop or a phone, in another country or language, and with many other factors. The related answer on why results look different for you and your customers explains why your own phone is a poor test.

As general advice, when a seller shows you a results page, ask which part they are talking about: a paid ad stops when the payment does, and an earned place cannot be bought.
