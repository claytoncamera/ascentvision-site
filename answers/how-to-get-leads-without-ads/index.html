How can a small business get leads without paying for ads?

Ascent Vision · Published October 15, 2026

A small business can get leads without ads from search, from AI answers, from referrals and past customers, from reviews, and from partners who serve the same customers. None of these is free, since each takes time, but none needs an ad budget. The key is to record where every inquiry came from, so you put your time into the channel that actually brings customers.

The channels

  • Search: being found on Google when customers look for your service.
  • AI answers: being named when customers ask ChatGPT, Perplexity or Google's AI who to hire.
  • Referrals: asking happy customers, directly, who else they know who needs you.
  • Past customers: a short note to people you have worked with, when you have something useful to say.
  • Reviews: recent, real reviews that help new customers choose you.
  • Partners: businesses that serve the same customers but do not compete with you.

Where to start

Start with what you already have: past customers and referrals are usually the quickest place to start, because those people already know you. In parallel, make sure your website says clearly what you do, where and for whom, and that every inquiry reaches you fast. Search and AI answers build over months, so starting early matters more than starting big.

How to know which works

Record the source of every inquiry and what happened to it. After three months, compare the quicker channels by customers won. Judge search and AI answers over a longer stretch, by early progress as well as customers. Put more into what works and less into the rest.
