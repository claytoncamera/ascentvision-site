How do ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google's AI answers, Gemini, Copilot and Claude find local businesses?

Ascent Vision · Published October 24, 2026

ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google's AI Overviews and AI Mode, Gemini, Copilot and Claude all say they can draw on the live web and show links or citations, with conditions that vary by plan and setting. Of the platform pages read on 2026-10-24, only Google's describe a listing a business can claim: its Business Profile. The others describe no way for a business to add or claim a listing. No platform is ranked here.

How to read this comparison

One row per assistant. Every cell comes from that platform's own help or developer page, quoted or closely paraphrased, and the pages are listed under Sources. Where a platform says yes, no or sometimes, the cell uses its words. Where the pages read do not answer the question, the cell says so. The rows are in no particular order, and nothing here ranks one assistant above another.

Google appears twice because it runs two different things: AI Overviews and AI Mode inside Google Search, and the separate Gemini app.

Re-check before you rely on this

Re-check: the platforms change; this table was read on 2026-10-24. Each row's pages are listed under Sources, so you can read the current version yourself. Microsoft's privacy FAQ says it covers only the older version of the Copilot app, which has since been updated (August 18, 2026), and Microsoft's page giving an overview of the Copilot app does not describe web search or citations. So the Copilot row combines that FAQ with Microsoft's page on web search in Copilot Chat and agents; read both before relying on it.

What this means for an owner

Across all six, the pattern is the same: the assistant reads what is on the web and, in most cases, shows where it looked. So say plainly what you do, where and for whom on your own website, and keep those facts current wherever you have put them. Answer the real questions customers ask, in their words, on your own pages, because that is text an assistant may quote. Keep reviews honest, and never pay or reward anyone for them. Then ask each assistant about your business and note what it says.
