How can I generate more leads for my service business?

Ascent Vision · Published October 24, 2026

Pull two levers: get more inquiries, and lose fewer of the ones you already get. Losing fewer usually comes first, since those people already asked: reply fast, follow up, cover after hours, and tag every inquiry with its source so you can repeat what works. For more inquiries, choose among eight channels (search, AI answer, social, referral, paid, email, campaign and direct) by what each takes from you and how soon it tends to pay.

Lever one: lose fewer of the inquiries you get

An inquiry you already have is often the cheapest to win. The related answer on what a lead system is covers how these habits fit together.

  • Reply fast: an alert the minute an inquiry lands, and a short first reply within the hour where you can.
  • Follow up: people often go quiet without meaning no, and a second message can bring them back.
  • Cover after hours: an automatic note saying when you will reply, sent by text only with the person's consent, and someone who keeps that promise.
  • Tag every inquiry with its source and its outcome, won, lost or still open; the related answer on lead attribution covers how.

Lever two: the eight lead channels

Judge every channel by inquiries tagged with it and followed to an outcome; what differs is the work and the wait. See the related answers on getting leads without ads, on lead generation companies (buying leads counts as paid) and on getting more reviews the right way.
