Should you hire a marketing agency or pay a lead generation company per lead?

Ascent Vision · Published October 30, 2026

A marketing agency is usually paid for the work, often monthly, and what it builds should sit in your name. A lead generation company is usually paid per lead and may keep the first contact, the phone number or the website. Neither is better for every business. Compare them by jobs won from each source: tag bought leads as paid with the seller's name, and agency work by the channel the lead came through.

The two models side by side

The related answer on what a lead generation company does covers the questions to ask a seller, and the one on what a marketing retainer is covers the monthly model. A results-based agency deal sits between the two; the related answer on pay-for-performance SEO covers it.

Whether leads are sharedInquiries usually arrive through your own site and accountsMay be exclusive or shared with other businesses; ask
What you keep when you stopYour accounts, site and records, if the agreement says soUsually the leads already received; the seller's pages and numbers may stay with it

How to compare them fairly

  • Keep one lead list with a source and an outcome on every lead; the related answers on lead generation companies and on buying leads from lead sites cover judging a seller by jobs won.
  • Tag leads from agency work by the channel they came through, such as search or AI answer, not as paid. The related answer on lead attribution lists the channels.

Which tends to fit

Per-lead buying may suit a business that needs inquiries for one kind of job in one area, can reply to shared leads quickly and has its own number and website for every customer. An agency may suit a business that wants a lasting online presence in its own name and can wait for it to build.

Some businesses use both. If you do, the tags above are what let you tell them apart. The related answer on who should own your business phone number explains why the number matters, whichever you choose. Ascent Vision is an agency and offers lead generation, so read this with that in mind.
