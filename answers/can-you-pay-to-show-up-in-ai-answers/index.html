Can you pay to show up in ChatGPT or Google's AI answers?

Ascent Vision · Published October 19, 2026

You can pay for an ad beside an AI answer, not for a place in the answer itself. Google shows labeled ads above, below or within AI Overviews, chosen by its normal ad auction, and says advertisers cannot target those placements directly. OpenAI says ads in ChatGPT are separate, clearly labeled and do not influence the answer. The written answer comes from a different system, and a paid recommendation inside it is not a product anyone can sell.

What Google sells around its AI answers

Google's Ads help page says ads are "eligible to be shown above, below or within AI Overviews on Search". They come from existing campaigns such as Search and Shopping and "serve following the existing auction ranking system and signals", the same auction that places ads on an ordinary results page. Google does not show them within overviews for sensitive areas such as healthcare, finance, gambling or politics.

Two details matter to an owner. Advertisers cannot choose this placement: "No, you can't directly target ad placements in the AI Overviews". And the ads are ads: Google's description of how Search works says they are clearly labeled so they are easy to tell apart from the rest of the page.

ChatGPT and other assistants

OpenAI set out its position in January 2026, when it said it planned to start testing ads in the U.S. for ChatGPT's free and Go tiers, with the Plus, Pro, Business and Enterprise plans ad-free. One of its stated principles for those ads is answer independence: "Ads do not influence the answers ChatGPT gives you", and "Ads are always separate and clearly labeled". The first format it described was an ad at the bottom of an answer when a relevant sponsored product or service fits the conversation, kept apart from the written answer and not shown near sensitive topics such as health, mental health or politics.

Formats, countries and tiers change, so check OpenAI's current published policy rather than a seller's summary. The part that matters to an owner is the stated principle: OpenAI says advertising does not decide what ChatGPT answers.

Why no one can sell a place in the answer itself

An AI answer is not a slot. It is text the assistant writes from what it can find about the businesses that fit the question, which is why it can name one business and skip another without anyone paying. Google's guidance for site owners says it "never accepts money to include or rank sites in our search results", and the related answer on whether Google Ads help your free rankings covers that for ordinary search.

That is why GEO is earned rather than bought: the related answer on how customers find businesses through AI assistants explains what an assistant needs to name a business correctly, and the related answer on what a GEO agency does describes the work as it should be sold.

What this means when someone sells you a spot

  • A promised place inside ChatGPT's or Google's written answer for a fee is a claim to test: ask which published policy page allows it.
  • Paid ads beside an AI answer are real, labeled, bought through the ordinary ad account, and stop when the budget stops.
  • A business can be named in the written answer without paying anything; that is the outcome GEO works toward.
  • Record inquiries that arrive from AI answers as their own channel, separate from paid, so you can see what each is worth.
