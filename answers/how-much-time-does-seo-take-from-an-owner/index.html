How much of an owner's time does SEO and GEO work take?

Ascent Vision · Published October 15, 2026

Usually less than owners expect, but never none. Most of the owner's part is knowledge only you have: answering questions about the business, approving what goes live, supplying real photos, asking customers for reviews and reading a short report each month. The first weeks often take more of your time; after that, an hour or two a month is often enough. The work tends to stall fastest when approvals sit waiting.

What only the owner can give

  • Answers about the business: what you do and do not do, where you work, how a job runs and what customers usually ask.
  • Approvals: someone who knows the business should check every fact before anything goes live in your name.
  • Real photos of your work, your team and your vehicles.
  • Review requests, sent to every customer when a job is finished.
  • A monthly read of the report, and a short call if something needs a decision.
  • News: a new service, a new area or a change in how you work.

Where the time usually goes

WhenWhat you doTime it often takes
Getting startedA longer talk about the business, sharing access to accounts you own, gathering photosSeveral hours, spread over the first weeks
Each monthApprove changes, read the reportAn hour or two
After each jobAsk the customer for a reviewA minute or two
When something changesTell whoever does the workA short message

How to keep it light

  • Name one person who can approve changes, so nothing waits on a busy week.
  • Set a fixed slot each week to clear approvals in one go.
  • Take photos at jobs as you go and drop them in one shared folder.
  • Answer questions by phone or voice note rather than writing long emails.
  • Use a saved message and a direct link for review requests. The related answer on getting more reviews the right way covers what to avoid.
  • Read the report with one question in mind: which inquiries came from where, and which became paid jobs.

Signs the balance is off

If you are spending hours each week writing or fixing pages yourself, you may be doing the specialist's job as well as your own. The related answer on how much of your marketing to do yourself covers what to keep and what to hand off.

If you never hear from whoever does the work and never approve anything, that is a warning sign too. Search results and AI answers can repeat what is published about you, so a wrong fact in your name can spread. A little of your time each month is what keeps it accurate.
