Do you have to pay to be listed for voice search, and are those offers real?

Ascent Vision · Published October 25, 2026

No. Several of the listings assistants such as Gemini, Siri and Alexa draw on can be claimed free. Google says a Business Profile is free and that Gemini uses public information from Google Maps to find places. Apple calls Apple Business a free service and says place cards appear in Siri. Amazon announced that Alexa+ would search partners such as Yelp and Angi for local home services, and Yelp says it is free to be on Yelp. A paid voice search registration usually sells what an owner can do free.

Where assistants get business details

Each maker describes its own source. The details below come from Google's, Apple's, Amazon's and Yelp's own pages; the related answer on whether Siri and Alexa recommend local businesses covers what those two assistants can actually do.

What a paid voice registration sells

As general advice, an offer to register a business for voice search is usually one of two things: someone claiming these free listings on your behalf, which an owner can do directly, or a promise of placement that no outside company can keep. No one can promise that any assistant will name a particular business.

The platforms do sell advertising. Apple says ads on Apple Maps are available in the United States and Canada, and Yelp offers paid upgrades alongside its free page. Buying an ad from a platform is a separate decision from being listed, and it does not make an outside seller's registration necessary.

How to check an offer

The FTC's guide for small businesses warns that scammers try to fool businesses into paying for nonexistent advertising or phony directory listings; the related answers on how to spot spam and scam inquiries, on how to spot an SEO scam and on paid directory listings cover its checks, including searching a company's name and bills for listings no one ordered.

As general advice, the quickest check is the platform's own page: if Google, Apple or Yelp says the listing is free, an outside fee usually buys convenience at most.

What is worth doing instead

Claim the free listings your customers actually use, keep hours and contact details current, and ask every customer for an honest review, without offering anything in return. The related answer on getting your business on Apple Maps covers Apple's side, and the related answer on what a Google Business Profile is covers Google's.
