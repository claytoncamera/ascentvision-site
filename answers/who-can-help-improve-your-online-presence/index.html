Who can help me improve my online presence and attract more clients?

Ascent Vision · Published October 24, 2026

Match the help to the problem. A web designer or developer fixes the site; an SEO or GEO agency or consultant works on being found on Google and named in AI answers; someone who sets up lead handling fixes inquiries that are lost, slow or untracked; a social media manager helps only if customers come from social. Owners can often do much of it alone. Insist that every inquiry is tagged with its source and that you own your domain, content and data.

Three questions that decide it

  • Where do customers look for you now? Ask your last ten customers how they found you; the most common answer is usually where help pays first.
  • What happens when they find you? Open your website on your phone as a stranger. If what you do or how to reach you is unclear, the site comes first.
  • What happens after they ask? If replies are slow, follow-up is patchy or you cannot say where inquiries came from, lead handling comes first.

Many owners need only one or two of these; the related answer on where a small business should start with marketing sets out an order. What Google says about hiring for search

Google's guide "Do you need an SEO?" says that if you run a small local business, "you can probably do much of the work yourself". It says "a great time to hire is when you're considering a site redesign". Its suggested interview questions include whether the SEO will share all the changes made to your site. It says to see whether the SEO is interested in your business: "If they're not interested, find someone who is." It also says no one can promise a number one ranking on Google.

It also suggests checking that any advice on optimizing for AI experiences (it names "AEO" and "GEO" services) is aligned with Google Search's official guidance. As general advice, its questions are a fair test for anyone you hire, not only for search.

What to insist on from anyone you hire

Whoever you hire, two things are not negotiable: every inquiry tagged with its source and followed to won, lost or still open, and your domain, content and data staying in your business's name. The related answer on questions to ask before hiring an SEO agency lists the rest, and the related answers on spotting an SEO scam, freelancer or agency, local or remote agencies and what a GEO agency does go further.
