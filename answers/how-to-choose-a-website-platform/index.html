How do you choose a website platform for a small business?

Ascent Vision · Published October 15, 2026

Choose the platform that lets you own your domain and content, edit the basics yourself, load fast on a phone, and leave without losing your site. Brand matters less than those four things. For most small service businesses the right choice is the simplest one that does all four at a cost you can carry for years. If you already have a site, check it against these four before you switch.

Four things that matter most

  • Ownership: the domain, the content and the accounts are in your business's name.
  • Editing: you can change text, photos and contact details yourself without paying someone.
  • Speed: pages load quickly on a phone on mobile data.
  • Leaving: you can take your content and move if you ever need to.

Also worth checking

  • The full yearly cost, including hosting, add-ons and renewals, not just the first month.
  • Whether forms deliver reliably, and where the messages go.
  • Who fixes it when something breaks, and how fast.
  • Whether it can grow: new service pages, new areas, a booking tool.

A warning sign

A platform or provider that keeps your domain in its own name, will not hand over your content, or charges to let you leave. Settle ownership before anything else.
