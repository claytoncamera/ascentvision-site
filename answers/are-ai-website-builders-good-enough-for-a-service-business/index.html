Is an AI website builder good enough for a service business?

Ascent Vision · Published October 25, 2026

It can be a fair start. Wix and Squarespace say their AI tools draft a site's content and design from details you give them. What no builder can supply is what makes a service business credible: your real services, area, license, photos and reviews, plus a form and phone number that reach you. Treat the result as a first draft, check every line, and make sure your business owns the domain before you commit.

What the builders say their AI does

Wix's help page on creating an AI-generated site says you answer the generator's questions in a chat, and the site's look and content are based on your business information. You can then adjust it, tweak the design or regenerate it. The same page says the AI Website Builder only creates new sites and cannot edit an existing one, though the editor has other AI tools.

Squarespace's help page says that when you create a site with its AI tools, including Blueprint AI Builder, the AI generates text and images and chooses style settings based on your prompts. It tells users to review all generated content because it "may not always be accurate, appropriate, or right for your needs", and says the AI may generate the same or similar content for you as for another user's site.

What still needs you

  • Your real services and the areas you cover. Squarespace's page itself says to edit generated text to add your voice or details unique to you.
  • License and insurance details, where they apply, checked against your records.
  • Real photos of your work and team in place of generated or stock images.
  • Reviews from real customers only.
  • A form that reaches a person and a tracked phone number, both tested after launch.

What Google says about AI-made pages

Google's position on AI-written content is covered in the related answer on whether AI can write your website; in short, the help is welcome and the checking is yours.

Owning it, leaving it, and the alternatives

Wix's page says a site made with the AI Website Builder cannot be switched to a different template; you would create a new site and can move your Premium plan to it. The related answers on moving a Wix or Squarespace website and on the GoDaddy website builder cover what can leave each platform.

As general advice, an AI draft can be enough for a few standard services in one area when you supply your own words and photos. A template you pick yourself gives more control over layout, and a designer usually makes sense for a custom quote or booking flow; the related answer on whether a website template is good enough covers that choice.
