GEO vs SEO: what is the difference, and do you need both?

Ascent Vision · Published October 14, 2026

SEO (search engine optimization) aims to get a business a ranked link on a search results page. GEO (generative engine optimization) aims to get it mentioned or recommended inside an answer written by an AI assistant. They share most of their foundations, but they are measured differently, and a business can do well in one while being absent from the other.

The difference in one table

SEOGEO
GoalA ranked link on a results pageA mention or recommendation inside an AI answer
Where it showsGoogle and other search enginesChatGPT, Perplexity, Google's AI Overviews and AI Mode, and other assistants
What the customer seesA list of links to choose fromA written answer, often naming only a few businesses
How you know it workedVisits and inquiries from searchMentions in AI answers and inquiries from them

Where they overlap

AI assistants and search engines read the same web. Google says there are no additional requirements to appear in its AI Overviews or AI Mode beyond what it asks of any page in search. A business that is clearly described and genuinely useful to its customers is in a better position for both.

Where they differ

A results page offers a page of choices; an AI answer usually names far fewer. The answer is written, so how a business is described matters as much as whether it appears. And because an assistant may name a business without anyone clicking, success is measured in mentions and in the inquiries that follow, not only in visits.

Do you need both?

For most service businesses, yes. Search is still where many customers start, and AI answers are where a growing number now start. Running both together, and tagging every lead with the channel it came from, shows which one is paying off for a particular business.
