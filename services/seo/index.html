Who it is for

Questions

How is Ascent Vision's SEO different from a typical SEO retainer?

Ascent Vision judges SEO on the inquiries it produces. Every lead from search is tagged with its source and followed to an outcome, so a client sees which searches turned into customers instead of a report of rankings and traffic.

Does SEO still matter now that people ask AI assistants?

Yes. Search results and AI answers draw on the same web, and a business that is easy to find and easy to understand in search is in a better position to be named in AI answers too. Ascent Vision runs SEO and GEO together for that reason.

How long before SEO shows results?

It depends on the market, the competition and where the site starts, so plan in months rather than weeks. Ascent Vision agrees what to measure at the start and reports it monthly, so progress is visible before it shows up as revenue.
