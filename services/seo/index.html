SEO for service businesses

SEO that brings in customers, not just visitors

Get found on Google by the people already looking for what you sell, in the places you serve, and see which searches turned into inquiries.

Ascent Vision's SEO service works to make a service business show up on Google when customers search for the services it sells, in the areas it serves. The work is judged on inquiries, not traffic: every lead that arrives from search is tagged with its source, so the business can see what search actually produced.

What you get

  • Built to be found on Google for the services you actually sell, not vanity terms
  • A clear page for each service you sell, written to turn a visit into an inquiry
  • Visible in the towns and areas you serve
  • A plain monthly read: where you show up, and which inquiries came from search

Who it is for

  • Service businesses and independent professionals whose customers search before they buy
  • Owners who want to know what their marketing produced, in leads, not in reports full of jargon
  • Businesses with a website that gets little or no search traffic today

What we will not do

  • Promise a position on Google. Nobody controls the ranking, and anyone who promises one is guessing.
  • Publish invented reviews, results or credentials on your behalf.
  • Hold your domain, your content or your data hostage. They are yours.

Questions

How is Ascent Vision's SEO different from a typical SEO retainer?

Ascent Vision judges SEO on the inquiries it produces. Every lead from search is tagged with its source and followed to an outcome, so a client sees which searches turned into customers instead of a report of rankings and traffic.

Does Ascent Vision do local SEO for small businesses?

Yes. Local SEO is the part of SEO that gets a business found by people searching for a service in the towns it serves. Ascent Vision's SEO service covers it for small service businesses and independent professionals, and tags every lead that arrives from search with its source.

Does SEO still matter now that people ask AI assistants?

Yes. Search results and AI answers draw on the same web, and a business that is easy to find and easy to understand in search is in a better position to be named in AI answers too. Ascent Vision runs SEO and GEO together for that reason.

How long before SEO shows results?

It depends on the market, the competition and where the site starts, so plan in months rather than weeks. Ascent Vision agrees what to measure at the start and reports it monthly, so progress is visible before it shows up as revenue.
