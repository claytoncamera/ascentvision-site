Websites, upgraded or built

Upgrade the site you have. Build new only when you must.

Most businesses do not need a new website. They need the one they have to load fast, say clearly what they do and ask the visitor to take one step.

Ascent Vision upgrades existing websites so they load fast on a phone, say clearly what the business does and ask for one action, and builds a new site only when the old one cannot be saved. The client owns the domain, the content and the data either way.

What you get

  • A review of what to keep, fix or replace, before any money goes into a rebuild
  • Fast and clear on a phone
  • Every page built around one action: an inquiry
  • You own the domain, the content and the data

Who it is for

  • Businesses whose site looks fine but produces few inquiries
  • Sites that are slow, unclear or hard to use on a phone
  • New businesses that need a first site built to be found

What we will not do

  • Rebuild a site that can be fixed.
  • Use stock photos or invented testimonials as proof.
  • Keep your domain or site in our name.

Questions

Should I upgrade my website or build a new one?

Upgrade when the site's address, content and platform are sound and the problems are speed, clarity or a missing call to action. Rebuild when the platform blocks basic changes, the site cannot be made fast on a phone, or the business has changed so much that most pages need rewriting.

Does Ascent Vision build on a specific platform?

Ascent Vision works with the platform a business already has where it can, and recommends a change only when the platform stands in the way of speed, clarity or being found.

Who owns the website when the work is done?

The client owns the domain, the content and the data. Ascent Vision does not keep a client's site or customers in its own name.
