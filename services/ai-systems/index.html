AI systems and automation

AI systems that take the repetitive work off your team

A lot of an owner's week goes to the same tasks: answering the same questions, chasing the same follow-ups, retyping the same details. We build the systems that do that work, so your people can spend their time on customers.

Ascent Vision builds AI systems and automations for businesses: the working pieces that take repetitive tasks off a team, such as capturing and routing inquiries, sending follow-ups, drafting quotes and documents, updating records and producing reports, plus AI agents that take steps within limits the business sets. Each system is built around one job, connected to the tools the business already uses, and set up so the business approves what it may say to customers and do on its own. The client owns the system's data and the accounts it runs on.

What you get

  • A look at your week first: which tasks are worth automating, and which are not
  • Systems built around one job each, such as intake, follow-up, scheduling, quotes or reporting
  • AI agents that can take steps, like booking or updating a record, inside limits you set
  • You approve what a system says to your customers and what it may do on its own

How it works

  1. Map the work

    We list the tasks that repeat, how long each takes and what goes wrong, then pick the ones worth automating.

  2. Build one job at a time

    Each system does one job well, is tested on your real examples and starts with a person approving its output.

  3. Connect and hand over

    It plugs into the tools you already use. You get a plain description of what it does, who can change it and how to switch it off.

  4. Watch it work

    We compare what it did with what it should have done, fix what is off and report in plain numbers.

Who it is for

  • Owners and managers who spend their evenings on admin
  • Teams that answer the same questions, or retype the same details, all week
  • Businesses where leads or jobs slip through because follow-up depends on memory

What we will not do

  • Promise a number of hours saved. It depends on the work, and we tell you what we measured.
  • Let a system email, text or charge a customer without your say-so, unless you ask for that.
  • Build something you cannot switch off.

Questions

What can be automated?

Work that follows the same steps each time: sorting and routing inquiries, sending follow-ups, creating records, drafting quotes, booking, reminders and reports. Work that depends on judgment, such as pricing a complicated job or handling an upset customer, stays with a person. The system prepares it and hands it over.

What is an AI agent?

An AI agent is a system that can take steps on its own, such as booking an appointment, updating a record or drafting a reply, inside limits a person sets. Ascent Vision builds agents with a person approving anything that reaches a customer until the business decides otherwise.

Do I need new software to use it?

Usually not. Ascent Vision connects to the tools a business already uses, such as email, calendars, forms, spreadsheets and accounting or job software. When a tool cannot be connected, or is the problem, we say so and recommend a replacement.

Where should a small business start with AI?

With one task that repeats every week and costs real time: the same question answered, the same follow-up chased, the same details retyped. We look at your week first, say which tasks are worth automating and which are not, and build around one job at a time.

Will an AI system send things to my customers without a person checking?

Only if you ask for that. You approve what a system may say to your customers and what it may do on its own, and nothing is built that you cannot switch off.
