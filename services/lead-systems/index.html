Lead generation and lead tracking

A lead system that gets every lead to you fast, with its source

A lead that waits a day goes cold, and a lead with no source teaches you nothing. Ascent Vision fixes both.

Ascent Vision's lead generation service for small service-based businesses has two halves. The first gets the business found, on Google and in AI answers, by the people already looking for what it sells. The second is the lead system: every inquiry is routed to the business as it arrives, recorded with where it came from (search, an AI answer, social, a referral, an ad, email, a campaign link or direct) and tracked to an outcome. The owner sees which channels bring in customers, not just clicks.

What you get

  • Inquiries sent to your phone as they arrive
  • Every lead tagged with where it came from
  • A simple board to work each lead from new to won or lost
  • A plain monthly read of which sources brought in customers

How it works

  1. Map how leads arrive

    Calls, forms, messages and referrals: where they land today and where they get lost.

  2. Route them fast

    Every inquiry is sent to the right person as it arrives, day or night.

  3. Tag every source

    Search, an AI answer, social, a referral, an ad or email, recorded with each lead.

  4. Work each to an outcome

    A simple board to take each lead to a yes or a no, and a plain monthly read.

Who it is for

  • Businesses that cannot say where their last ten customers came from
  • Owners who lose leads in inboxes, DMs and voicemail
  • Anyone about to spend on marketing who wants to know what it produces

What we will not do

  • Report a number the records do not show.
  • Sell or share your leads. Your lead data is yours.
  • Lock your lead history into a tool you cannot leave.

Questions

Does Ascent Vision do lead generation for small businesses?

Yes. Ascent Vision generates leads for small service businesses and independent professionals by getting them found on Google and in AI answers, then tracks every lead that arrives back to its source. It does not buy or sell lead lists, and it does not promise a number of leads.

What is a lead system?

A lead system is the path an inquiry takes from the moment a customer reaches out to the moment it ends in a yes or a no: where it arrives, who is alerted, how fast it is answered, where it is recorded and what source it is credited to.

What sources does Ascent Vision tag leads with?

Every lead is sorted into one channel: AI answer, Search, Social, Referral, Paid, Email, Campaign or Direct, together with the page it started on, so the business can compare what each channel produced.

Who owns the lead data?

The client. Ascent Vision records leads to show where they came from and what happened to them; it does not sell or share a client's leads, and returns or deletes them on request, apart from backups and records the law requires it to keep.

How do I know which marketing channels are bringing in customers?

Record where every inquiry came from and what happened to it, then read the count by channel each month. Ascent Vision's lead system sorts every lead into one of eight channels (search, AI answer, social, referral, paid, email, campaign or direct) and follows it to won, lost or still open, so the owner sees which channels bring customers, not just visits.
