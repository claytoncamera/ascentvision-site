AI assistants and chatbots

An assistant that answers from your information and hands off to a person

A good assistant answers the question a customer asks at nine at night, in your words, from your information, and brings in a person when it should. We build that, and we test it on your real questions before a customer sees it.

Ascent Vision builds AI assistants for businesses: chat assistants that answer customers on a website or by text, voice assistants that answer the phone, and staff assistants that answer questions about policies, products and procedures, all from the business's own information. Each assistant is told what it may and may not say, is tested on real questions before launch and hands off to a person when a question is outside what it should answer, when the topic is sensitive or when the customer asks.

What you get

  • A customer assistant that answers from your own information, on your site, by text or by phone
  • A staff assistant that finds answers in your policies, product details and procedures
  • Clear limits: what it may say, what it should not say and when it hands off to a person
  • Conversations kept in a log you can read

How it works

  1. Define the job and the limits

    What the assistant answers, what it never says and when it brings in a person.

  2. Give it your information

    Your services, policies and common questions, written down once, in your words.

  3. Test it on real questions

    We run the questions your customers actually ask and fix every answer that is wrong or off-brand before launch.

  4. Launch, then read the conversations

    You see what people asked, and we correct and extend the assistant as real questions come in.

Who it is for

  • Businesses that get the same questions all day, or miss messages after hours
  • Teams where new staff keep asking the owner how things are done
  • Owners who tried a chatbot, did not trust it and want one that stays in its lane

What we will not do

  • Set an assistant loose without limits. It is set up to answer only from information you approve, and we review its conversations.
  • Build an assistant to pass as a person. It is set up to say it is an assistant when asked, and to say so up front where the rules or you require it.
  • Switch on recording, texting or calling before you confirm the consent that applies to your customers. This is not legal advice.

Questions

Will an AI assistant say something wrong about my business?

It can, which is why we limit it. The assistant is set up to answer only from information you approve, is told what it should not say and is tested on real questions before launch. We read its early conversations with you, correct what it got wrong and keep checking after launch.

Can an assistant answer the phone?

Yes. A voice assistant can answer calls, take details, book or hand off to a person. Whether it fits depends on who calls and on the rules about recording calls, which are covered before anything is built.

Will an assistant replace my staff?

The aim is to take the repeated questions so your people have more time for the conversations that need them. The assistant hands off anything that needs judgment, a decision or a human voice.

Where does the assistant get its answers?

From your own information, approved by you: your services, hours, policies and procedures. It is set up to answer only from that information and to hand off to a person when it should, and its conversations are kept in a log you can read.

Can an assistant book appointments?

It can book only into a calendar it can actually see, for the job types and time slots you set, and it tells the person what happens next. Anything it is unsure about goes to a person.
