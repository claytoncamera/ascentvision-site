Privacy notice

What we collect

Only what you type or pick on the Work with us application: your name, email and phone, the business and what it does, the area it serves, its website, what you need, how customers find you today, the problem you want solved, when you want to start, how you would like to be contacted, how you heard about us, and anything you add in the message box. While you fill it in, your answers are kept in your own browser so a refresh does not lose them; they are removed from it when you send.

We also record how you reached the site (for example a search, an AI answer, an ad or a link) so we know which work brings people in.

Why

To reply to your application, prepare for the first call, and measure which of our marketing produced it. We do not sell your information and we do not share it for anyone else's marketing.

Who sees it

The two founders of Ascent Vision LLC. The alert that tells us an application arrived carries no name or contact details.

How long we keep it

For as long as it is useful for the project, and no longer than two years after our last contact with you.

Deleting it

Ask us to delete your information at any time through the form on the Work with us page.
